# Restaurant CRM: Inventory and Stock

This source covers inventory workflow guidance. Stock quantity, purchase price, expiry date, and reorder status must always be read from the live inventory database.

## Inventory items
Each ingredient or sellable stock item should have:
- item name and category
- unit: kg, gram, litre, ml, piece, packet, bottle, or box
- current stock quantity
- minimum/reorder level
- supplier
- purchase price and optional expiry/batch details

## Stock operations
- Stock in: record received quantity, supplier, invoice/reference, purchase cost, and expiry/batch where needed.
- Stock out/adjustment: use only for wastage, damage, staff consumption, correction, or physical-count mismatch. Record a reason.
- Physical stock count: compare actual count with system count and create an auditable adjustment only after verification.
- Never tell a customer or manager an exact stock value unless it comes from the live system.

## Low stock and Smart Reorder
Smart Reorder keeps a configured list of items and reorder quantities for the restaurant.
When stock reaches the configured threshold, review the suggested quantity, supplier availability, recent consumption, and existing purchase orders before ordering.
A low-stock alert is a signal to review; it is not proof that an item is unavailable to sell.

## Menu mapping
Menu items can map to one or more inventory ingredients. Correct mapping prevents sales of a dish from being shown as available when required ingredients are unavailable.
If deductions look wrong, verify item unit conversion, recipe quantity, and duplicate/missing mappings.

## Supplier support
Use supplier records for supplier name, contact, supplied items, expected delivery, and past order references.
Before creating a purchase order, confirm the supplier and quantity with an authorized manager.
