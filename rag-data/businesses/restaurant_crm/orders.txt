# Restaurant CRM: Orders and Kitchen Workflow

## Order flow
Typical states are:
1. Pending or new order
2. Accepted / sent to kitchen
3. Preparing
4. Ready to serve or packed
5. Completed after payment/closure
6. Cancelled when the order is not fulfilled

Use the actual status configured by the restaurant. Do not change an order status without staff authorization.

## Dine-in orders
A dine-in order must be linked to the correct table number and, where available, floor or section.
Before adding items, confirm the table. For changes, repeat the item and quantity back to staff or customer.

## Takeaway and delivery
Capture customer name, contact number, items, quantity, delivery/takeaway preference, address if applicable, and payment status.
Never expose another customer's order, phone number, address, or payment information.

## Cancellation and modification
- Before kitchen acceptance: update or cancel after confirming the request.
- After preparation starts: escalate to restaurant staff because food may already be prepared.
- A cancellation reason should be recorded for reporting.
- Refunds or payment reversals require authorized staff and payment verification.

## WhatsApp ordering
Customer messages can be used to show Today's Special, Full Menu, and order options.
The final order should be confirmed with item, quantity, price, delivery/table details, and total before placing it.
