# Restaurant CRM: Billing and Payments

## Bill handling
A bill should show ordered items, quantity, item price, applicable taxes/charges, discounts if authorized, and final payable amount.
Check the live order before quoting the final bill. Do not calculate or invent totals from memory.

## Payment
Accepted payment modes may include cash, UPI, card, or other configured methods.
Mark payment completed only after staff confirms payment success.
For UPI/card failures, duplicate debits, refund requests, or charge disputes, collect order/reference details and hand off to an authorized payment support person.

## Discounts and complimentary items
Only authorized users may apply discounts, coupons, or complimentary items.
Record the reason and authorization where the Restaurant CRM workflow requires it.

## Privacy
Never send payment reference, phone number, invoice details, or order history to an unverified person.
